# Capstone Project: Vilnius Public Transport Passenger Traffic Analysis & Optimization

## 👤 Author
**Artsiom Padmaska**

## 📝 Abstract
This capstone project explores passenger traffic patterns in Vilnius public transportation using comprehensive data analysis techniques. The project aims to enhance public transport efficiency by identifying peak usage times, optimizing ticketing strategies, and recommending dynamic pricing models based on demand and travel time analysis.

## 🎯 Project Goals
- Analyze and visualize passenger traffic data to identify peak and off-peak usage.
- Assess the efficiency of current ticket validity strategies.
- Propose optimized ticketing solutions, including dynamic pricing and flexible validity periods.
- Improve passenger satisfaction and transportation system revenue.

## Hypotheses
1. Flexible ticket validity periods with distinct time slots can better accommodate diverse passenger travel patterns.
2. Dynamic pricing strategies based on real-time demand and travel time significantly reduce congestion during peak periods and enhance revenue management.

## Data Sources
- GTFS (General Transit Feed Specification) Data
- Passenger Flow Data (Monthly and Hourly Passenger Traffic)

## Methodology
### Data Analysis
- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and visualization of passenger flows
- Identification of high-traffic stops and analysis of usage patterns

### Optimization Techniques
- Calculation of optimal validity periods for tickets based on passenger flow patterns
- Statistical analysis (weighted mean, median, percentile analysis)
- Formulation of dynamic pricing models, incorporating real-time demand and travel duration

## 📋 Results and Findings
- Clear identification of peak and off-peak travel periods.
- Recommended optimal validity periods and dynamic pricing scenarios based on passenger behavior.
- Identified potential for balanced resource utilization and revenue optimization.

## 📑 Presentation Materials
- Traffic heatmaps, peak-time analyses, and dynamic pricing models visualizations
- Comprehensive analysis report (PDF format)

## Future Directions

- Integration of additional datasets for extended analysis
